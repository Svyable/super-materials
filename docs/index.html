Why quenchability matters

Ambient pressure ≠ ambient-temperature survival

Pressure-quenched Hg1223 reaches 151 K at ambient pressure, while its retained state degrades on warming. Hydrides independently demonstrate that metastable hydrogen-rich phases can sometimes be recovered. The research problem is therefore two-dimensional: pairing and retention.

Hg1223

151 K retained superconductivity

AMBIENT PRESSURE · THERMALLY FRAGILE

Pressure quench from 18.9 GPa at 4.2 K retained an ambient-pressure Tc up to 151 K. Related states survived 77 K storage for at least three days, while warming toward room temperature degraded retained Tc.

Replication standard

One trace is the beginning.

The same-state rule requires structure, transport and later magnetic/thermodynamic evidence to be tied to a reproducible material state: pressure, temperature, isotope, thermal history, elapsed time and precursor route.

Anomaly

Resistance, susceptibility, diffraction or spectroscopy feature.

Transport-consistent

Four-probe transition, field suppression, current checks.

Phase-linked

Structural fingerprint on the same state as transport.

Bulk-consistent

Shielding/Meissner and/or thermodynamic anomaly.

Mechanism-resolved

Isotope, gap, α²F or critical-field systematics.

Independent replication

Another lab reproduces phase, transition and controls.

Room-temperature-superconductivity language is reserved for level 6. Controversial and preprint claims remain visible below, but they do not silently inherit the status of independently reproduced results.
Quantum criticality

Search the trajectory, not the point.

H3S now provides a concrete 2026 example: its displacive quantum critical point is calculated near 134 GPa, while the experimental Tc maximum lies in the neighboring high-fluctuation regime. That motivates pressure/composition/isotope trajectories rather than single harmonic structures.

Gate 1

Structural free energy

Track quantum-renormalized curvature and phase competition, not only harmonic imaginary modes.

Gate 2

Mode-resolved coupling

Ask which soft and H-rich modes contribute to α²F and whether ωlog collapses as λ rises.

Gate 3

Defects + isotope

Separate critical softening from H loss, vacancy diffusion and phase mismatch.
